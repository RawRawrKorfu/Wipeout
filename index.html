🪙 0  ·  coin multiplier ×1  ·  ride:

Every key is rebindable in the Controls tab (defaults shown here). D/A run · S slide (under red blocks) · W/Space jump — press jump right as you land for a BHOP (coins + speed). Hold E and flick the mouse in the air: up = frontflip, down = backflip, right = spin, left = superman. Hold Q or F + flick for skateboard/scooter/freestyle tricks. Double jump (Parkour tab): press jump again in the air. Roll: tap Roll (or slide) just before landing from a drop. Slide-hop: jump, tap/hold slide as you land, jump again to keep your bhop chain. X = cash out (ground or air, but not once you're falling into a pit). The mouse is captured while playing (click the game if Esc released it). Land upright to bank tricks. Fall = lose everything you carried.
